The voice that comes over the screen at the daily recap. *Crawlers, welcome to the dungeon.
It's so good to have you here.* She goes on for another five or ten minutes about odd things
the party doesn't recognise, and [[mauritius]] tries to keep up, scratching notes onto a slate
with a fingernail. She files the slates on a haphazard stack in the corner.

## The Session 03 recap

- A levelling bug has been patched. Everyone now has the level they earned — for kills, for bosses and for grinding.
- Unassigned characteristic points won't matter until the third floor. Keep banking them.
- Some cheeky crawlers found a way past the skill cap on the first floor. *Click* — they were patched back down.
- Find the stairwell. *Kill, kill, kill.*

Mauritius's verdict on the patch: *that's what they do here. Don't let them see how good
something is, or they'll take it away from you. They did it to me.*

She also said the recap happens every day — *that sadistic son of a bitch gets on and gives
us some shit.*
