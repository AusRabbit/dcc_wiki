pants line on an Australian beach, which the GM noted is not a thing you find on an
Australian beach.

Jammed at point-blank range against [[dale-prosky]] at the worst possible moment — the GM's
ruling being that the weapon had been down a trucker's trousers for an unknown length of
time and the maintenance had not been smart.
