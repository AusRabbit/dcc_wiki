Range 20 to 30 feet, two hands to wield, 1d10 piercing. Was stitched into [[darcy-smith]]'s
pants line on an Australian beach, which the GM noted is not a thing you find on an
Australian beach.

Jammed at point-blank range against [[dale-prosky]] at the worst possible moment — the GM's
ruling being that the weapon had been down a trucker's trousers for an unknown length of
time and the maintenance had not been smart.

## Betsy

Darcy's gun is called Betsy.

In [[session-02]] she turned a rat into a spray of gore, missed point-blank at the back of
[[the-bone-collector]]'s head, and then — *come on, Betsy* — rolled a natural 20. A crit
doubles the damage dice, so four d10s: 23 damage, through the head and out the back. Darcy
kissed her first.

**Ammo is running low.** She found nothing to kill during the six-hour grind home. Along the
way, [[frank-doyle]] fixed her trigger, which had been a bit too sensitive.
