A door found round a corner at a dead run, marked with words to the effect of *Tutorial
Guild*. Inside, something between a health retreat and a share-house lounge room: bead
curtains, beanbags, yoga mats spread about, shoes off at the door, incense heavy in the air,
and a powerful smell of body odour.

[[mauritius]] hangs from the ceiling. It's a safe room — short rest, full heal, loot boxes and
skills — and according to her it's more like a portal: there are many doors and many
entrances. The party spent four hours here in [[session-01]] before she threw them out.

Rules advice from the GM now arrives as flashbacks to this room.
