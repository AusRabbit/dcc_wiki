American, driving through Noosa, stopped for a few bevvies and a rest before getting back
on the lonely road. Used to ride motorcycles, and became a trucker because he loves the
open road and stopping wherever he likes. He knows people. He won't say who or where, but
it's why getting a gun was never a problem. He says he has no enemies. Was on the beach
with a beer when the world ended, with a [[sawn-off-shotgun]] stitched into his pants line.

Charismatic and quick, and not at all clever — dexterity six, charisma five, strength four,
constitution three, intelligence two, and openly uninterested in magic. He considers himself
the natural leader of the team.
