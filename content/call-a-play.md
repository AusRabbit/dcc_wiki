---

Spend an action to call a play: roll 2d6, keep the highest, and add it to an ally's roll when
they do the thing you called. If they would have succeeded anyway, it goes on their damage
instead. It's a hit-back action (beat 4 of a [[combat]] round), not a reaction. The play needs
a name, and the name sticks.

**The Looky-loo** — [[frank-doyle]] gets the enemy's attention, [[darcy-smith]] shoots them.
Established on the beach on the way to [[the-stairwell]] and called twice in the first
fight. [[frank-doyle]]'s preferred name for it was rejected.

**The Reverse Looky-loo** — Darcy gets their attention and Frank does the damage. Floated
against the goblins in [[session-01]] and found again in this wiki mid-fight. Not yet called:
Frank went with a taunt instead.
