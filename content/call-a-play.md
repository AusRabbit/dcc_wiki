Spend an action to call a play: roll 2d6, keep the highest, and add it to an ally's roll. The
play needs a name, and the name sticks.

**The Looky-loo** — [[frank-doyle]] gets the enemy's attention, [[darcy-smith]] shoots them.
Established on the beach on the way to [[the-stairwell]] and called twice in the first
fight. [[frank-doyle]]'s preferred name for it was rejected.
