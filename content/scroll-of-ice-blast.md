One shot, no mana. It casts [[spell-ice-blast|Ice Blast]] at rank 5 as though Frank had cast
it himself, then crumbles to ash. Damage is 2d8 + Int, but he has to roll Int to hit first.

[[frank-doyle]] read it at [[the-bone-collector]] in [[session-02]] and rolled an 11 — a
standard fail, with no AI favour left to reroll. It missed the boss and froze most of the
floor instead, leaving a cold, slick surface everyone struggled on. Frank argued that the
rats should now have Disadvantage. The GM ruled the ice was just as hard for the party, so it
was an even playing field.
