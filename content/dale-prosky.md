Twenty minutes after his species received formal notice of its own extinction, Dale found a
staircase, stood in front of it, and began charging admission. He did not own the staircase.
He did not own anything. He did own a two-foot length of timber with nails through it, and
he stood between the survivors of a dead world and their only way out.

In Dale's mind this was a completely functional and logical road for his life.

Visibly under the influence, freakishly strong for it, and erratic. Killed [[cheryl]] and at
least one other before the party arrived, then took [[michelle]] hostage. Took a point-blank
shotgun shot that missed, several club hits, and a beer can to the head.

Died on the way down [[the-stairwell]], underneath the scrum, with his neck at an ungodly
angle.

> And this is the one your planet chose to save. The GM's introduction, delivered as a
> broadcast warm-up act — the first sign that the show is watching.
