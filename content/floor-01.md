Stone corridors lined with green lichen that gives off just enough light to see by. Every
intersection leads to another intersection. The party arrived soaked in blood within their
first hour.

[[borant-corporation]]'s entry announcement recommends immediately finding and using a
tutorial guild — advice that is easier given than followed when every corridor looks like
the last one.
