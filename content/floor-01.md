Stone corridors lined with green lichen that gives off just enough light to see by. Caged
doorways and barricaded sections. Every intersection leads to another intersection that
looks exactly the same. The party arrived soaked in blood within their first hour.

[[borant-corporation]]'s entry announcement recommends immediately finding and using a
tutorial guild — advice that is easier given than followed when every corridor looks like
the last one.

A countdown timer in the HUD shows how long is left before the floor collapses. Cheering and
other suspicious activity echo from somewhere in the distance. The floor's mess is cleaned up
by its janitor mobs, the [[rats]], and it ends up in [[the-sump]], a sewer sub-level that
the party found behind a broken grate.

This is a tutorial floor: you gain skills here by trying things ([[levelling-skills]]).
