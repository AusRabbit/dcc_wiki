[[darcy-smith]] was on the beach at Noosa with a beer and a sawn-off shotgun down his
pants. [[frank-doyle]] was there to clear his head about dropping out of university.
Neither knew the other. At 9pm the world flattened.

## The Flattening

A rush of dust came off the ruined boulevard and onto the sand. The couple who had been
canoodling under a beach awning a minute earlier rolled onto the beach as a pair of
bodiless legs. Then the text appeared in front of every surviving face, in a language
nobody had learned but everybody could read: the planet had been seized under
[[the-syndicate]]'s precious elemental reserves code, and [[borant-corporation]],
assigned regency, had chosen option three — [[the-world-dungeon]], eighteen levels deep.

## Two Strangers and a Light

The pair met over a beer and a blueberry-ice vape, discovered a shared broken-family
history and some shared politics, and set off down the beach towards the only light left
standing. Hundreds of people were screaming in the dark. The light never seemed to get
closer.

## The Stair Bully

What they found at [[the-stairwell]] was [[dale-prosky]] at the top of the steps with a
nailed two-by-four, standing over bodies and a small cache of stolen goods, charging
admission to the end of the world. He had just pulled the timber out of the back of a
woman's skull — [[cheryl]], as it turned out — and he grabbed her cousin [[michelle]] as a
hostage when the pair approached.

[[darcy-smith]] threw a beer can at his head and tried to trade him a truck full of
supplies. [[frank-doyle]] called the party's first play — [[call-a-play|the Looky-loo]] —
and went round his blind side with a club. It went less cleanly than either intended:
[[michelle]] grabbed at the shotgun in the scrum, the gun jammed at point-blank range, and
with under a minute before the stairwell sealed the whole tangle went down the stairs in a
heap.

[[dale-prosky]] arrived at the bottom with his neck at an ungodly angle.

## Floor One

Through an ornate set of doors worked with something demonic and aquatic, a booming voice
welcomed the crawlers to [[floor-01]]. Green lichen, endless junctions, and children's
laughter coming from ahead and behind at once. Twice they glimpsed
[[the-white-dress-girls]] standing in the middle distance, gone when looked at properly.

[[frank-doyle]] spent half an hour patting the walls looking for a door. Then the rumbling
started, and [[the-wall-of-blood]] came round the corner like a red avalanche.
[[darcy-smith]] climbed the wall, hung off a brazier and held [[michelle]] over one
shoulder. [[frank-doyle]] tried to surf it and got slammed into a wall.

Drenched and coughing, they rounded the corner, found a door marked *Tutorial Guild*, and
fell through it into [[tutorial-guild]] — incense, yoga mats, body odour, and a bat-like
creature unfolding from the ceiling to introduce herself as [[mauritius]].

Session ends there, on a short rest and a full heal.
