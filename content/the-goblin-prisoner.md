The one [[goblin-searchers|searcher]] left after the fight in [[session-01]]. [[darcy-smith]]
intimidated him with a natural 20, and he handed over his warrant with trembling hands:
*please kindly sir, I must finish my mission.*

His chief sent the searchers out to recover *that which is most precious* and put the
sisters to rest. He reckons the dead colleague who had the best lead was the one about to
tell them something important. It was his idea to follow the [[rats]].

## What he's said about the sisters

- They've been dead for ages. Now they're up, walking around and causing mayhem.
- Get their bones back to the grave, and they might quit.
- The grave is nowhere near [[the-sump|the bone pit]]. He could take them later. *Mind you, last time we went to the graves, things got wild.*
- On Darcy's two-hour bone: *could be. Not sure.*

## In the bone pit

He wanted no part of [[the-bone-collector]]. Mid-fight he bolted for the sealed portcullis
and spun [[michelle]] around so hard she lost the rope and fell into the pit. He was working
on untying himself when the boss died.

After the fight, the GM offered a zinger: you've got a goblin right here — how would you work
out what goblin bones look like? Darcy suggested skinning him. The party decided against it.
*Are we murderers yet?*

Before that, [[frank-doyle]]'s backup plan had been to leave him for the rats if the sewer
got dicey.
