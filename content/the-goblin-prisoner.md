The one [[goblin-searchers|searcher]] left after the fight in [[session-01]]. [[darcy-smith]]
intimidated him with a natural 20, and he handed over his warrant with trembling hands:
*please kindly sir, I must finish my mission.*

His chief sent the searchers out to recover *that which is most precious* and
put the sisters to rest. He reckons the dead colleague who had the best
lead was the one about to tell them something important. It was his idea to follow the
[[rats]].

He is tied up and walking with the party. He seems to think being helped is the best thing
that has ever happened to him, and he is glad to get away from the giggling. He has been
useful so far — steering them off bad turnings, and warning them about his own trap, a
stick propped up on another stick.

[[frank-doyle]]'s backup plan is to leave him for the rats if the sewer gets dicey.
