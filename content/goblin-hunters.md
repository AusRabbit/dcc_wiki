Green-skinned and cheeky, in a mishmash of gear. The loincloth and war paint aren't theirs.
They came from asset pack number seven, *noble primitive*, and the goblins hate it. They
have asked repeatedly to have their normal gear back, and been told the theme stays.

Each carries a boomerang that makes the round trip. When it misses, it clatters off the walls
and keeps flying back for another go.

- **Attack** — boomerang, ranged, 1d6+2.
- **Armour** — at least one wears a crockery helmet, which turned [[frank-doyle]]'s paddle twice.

## Encountered

Three of them, alongside three [[goblin-searchers]], at a rubble-strewn intersection on
[[floor-01]]. See [[session-01]].

- One turned to red pulp by [[frank-doyle]]'s first Magic Missile.
- One had its leg broken and then its head knocked off by [[michelle]].
- One ran screaming into the tunnels. The GM was clear it will probably come back.
