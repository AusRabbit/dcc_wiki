In [[session-03]], [[darcy-smith]] shot a [[shillelagh-bruiser]] with a natural 20. The bruiser
exploded, and its club spun through the air into Darcy's hands.

- **Damage** — 1d8 + Str, bludgeoning. The GM admits it might be a bit overpowered, and he'll wing it.
- **Skill** — Club.

It's the weapon [[mauritius]] would approve of: low-powered, hand-to-hand, and good for AI
favour. Darcy used it to finish a bruiser in the [[leprechaun-quarter]] and to hit back during
the fight with [[fergal-goldgrip]].

> [!note] Weapons and skills
> The GM's explanation: the weapon is a glove on the skill. A bat, a stick and this bludger
> all use Club. Getting better at Club makes all of them better, but the bludger itself
> never levels up. See [[combat]].
