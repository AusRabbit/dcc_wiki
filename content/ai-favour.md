[[mauritius]]'s explanation, in a flashback during [[session-02]]: the System AI is a dick.
When she was a crawler, it kept score and played favourites. Push your luck too far and
eventually it runs out.

It's on the party dashboard. Hover over **AI Favour** to read the details. Humans start
with 1.

## Spending it

One point gets you either of these:

- **A reroll** — once per check, reroll the d20 *before the GM announces the result*. You must keep the new roll. You can't reroll a natural 1. That's a Critical Fail, and the AI wants to see what happens.
- **An extra action** — once per round in combat, take an extra action that isn't an attack.

Speak up quickly. Darcy asked the GM to slow down so he had time to decide. Once the result
has been announced, it's too late.

The GM also lets you spend it to change the map, for example to put a pillar where you need
one, because there are pillars elsewhere.

## Earning it

Do something heroic, survive something foolish, stick it to the showrunners, or entertain the
AI. Mauritius's advice: the crowd doesn't want a Magic Missile on a rat. They want you in
there with your teeth and fingernails. Wild, risky moves earn favour, and low-powered attacks
earn it too.

- [[darcy-smith]] earned one for climbing [[the-bone-collector]] to feed it a [[biscuit-bomb]].
