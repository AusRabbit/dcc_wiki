The party stood on the rim of [[the-sump|the bone pit]] with a goblin on a rope, wondering how
you find one particular set of bones in forty metres of them. [[frank-doyle]] decided to
investigate up close, and that's when the edge gave way.

## Kitted Out

New loot from the safe room. [[darcy-smith]] swapped the poncho for a
[[motorcycle-jacket]] that looks much cooler and does exactly the same job, and picked up
the [[golf-club-of-the-moist-middle-manager]]. Frank got a [[tire-iron]] he can't use well
and a [[short-bow]] he can't use at all. Darcy handed Frank [[moms-emergency-poncho]] for his
bare torso, plus a mana potion, and Frank gave Darcy one of his two poison antidotes after a
small fight with the inventory screen.

## Into the Pit

[[the-goblin-prisoner]] explained what he wanted. The sisters have been dead for ages, and
now they're up, walking around causing mayhem. Get their bones back to the grave and they
might quit. The grave is nowhere near here, he added. The last time they went to the
graves, things got wild.

Frank stepped up to the rim to look closer. He rolled a 20 and a 1 with Disadvantage, so
the 1 counted. The brickwork crumbled and he went clattering down onto a rib cage. The
poncho held.

Then time froze. *BOSS BATTLE* streamed across everyone's HUD, the bones erupted, and
[[the-bone-collector]] climbed out — a rat taller than a man, wearing other things' bones as
armour. Portcullis grates slammed down behind them. They were sealed in the pit until they
won or died.

## The Bone Collector

[[rats]] flanked the party while the boss burrowed out of sight. Frank, explaining that he'd
read all the manga for exactly this moment, tried to leap onto a stone pillar. He fumbled the
jump, scrambled up anyway, drew the short bow, and fumbled the shot so badly the bow ended up on
the ground at his feet. Darcy jumped into the pit and turned the nearest rat into a spray of gore. *Hasta la
vista, rat.*

The Bone Collector burst out of the ground beside Darcy in a shower of shrapnel bone while two
rats climbed the pillar after Frank and got under the poncho. Darcy had an idea: crawler
biscuits plus dynamite. He [[biscuit-bomb|fabricated a biscuit bomb]] mid-fight on a
Disadvantage roll and made it. Frank hit the boss with a Magic Missile. Behind them, the
goblin bolted for the portcullis, spun [[michelle]] around, and she lost the rope and fell
into the pit onto something sharp.

Then the plan. Darcy let the boss slam him into the wall — staggered, and he admits he was
hoodwinked — then climbed it. He missed the climb by a whisker. Frank spent an action
shouting *put your leg on his other arm!*, Darcy got up onto its shoulders, and he rammed the
biscuit bomb down its throat. He spent his AI favour to make the roll and chose to succeed
with a consequence. It bit his hand badly and gave him a minor injury, but it ate the bomb.
Ten damage, and its entrails came out. It was still very much alive.

Michelle got back up and set a rat on fire with a spell nobody knew she had — Fire
Fingers. [[mauritius]] came back to them in a flashback: the System AI is a dick, and back when she
was a crawler it kept score and played favourites, and crowds don't want overkill on small mobs, they want teeth and fingernails. The GM
gave Darcy an AI favour on the spot for the climb. Frank took the hint, kicked the rat
gnawing his toe up to eye level like a hacky sack and smashed it with the tire iron.

It got worse before it got better. Darcy's point-blank shot to the back of the head missed,
and the Bone Collector threw him off into the bones up to his chest. He drank a gold standard
healing potion to get back to full. Frank read his [[scroll-of-ice-blast]] and missed,
freezing the whole floor instead. Michelle dodged the boss behind a pillar and let it charge
into the stone.

Then Darcy kissed his shotgun. *Come on, Betsy.* Natural 20, four d10s, twenty-three
damage, through the head and out the back. The Bone Collector made a sound halfway between
a roar and a whimper, and fell dead into its own pit. The rest of the rats fled.

*How do you like that, Frankie? Toss me the vape.*

## Michelle Catches Fire

The celebration lasted about as long as the vape. Michelle screamed: her Fire Fingers had
run up her arms and set her torso alight. Frank jumped down — another fumbled jump — and
emptied his water over her. The fire went out, and so did the poncho, which went up in
flames and left Frank with burns across his chest. Michelle was down to her last health
slot. Frank gave her his healing potion.

## The Right Bones

The portcullis opened and the announcer called it. They looted a
[[neighbourhood-map|neighbourhood map]], which spilled across their minimaps, and a
[[bone-carapace]] that might become armour one day.

That left the bones. Darcy tracked where the Bone Collector had spent most of its time,
rolled a natural 1, and spent two hours certain about a small bone that the goblin looked at
and called *could be, not sure*. He apologised for being a shitty leader today. The GM
offered a zinger — you've got a goblin right here, how would you work out what goblin bones
look like? The party considered it and declined. *Are we murderers yet?*

Frank went dumpster diving instead. Two more hours, but he came up with one of the bones.
More rats were arriving by the minute, wondering where their leader had gone, and the party
were the only meat in the room. Four hours gone.

## Grinding Home

With the neighbourhood map, they ground for six hours on the way back to the safe room
([[levelling-skills]]). Frank's magic missiles found plenty to kill. Darcy's shotgun found
nothing — he's running low on ammo — so he experimented with biscuit bombs instead. Frank
fixed Betsy's touchy trigger along the way.

[[mauritius]] was waiting at [[tutorial-guild]], wings spread and standing on one leg. *My
little bat-lings! A huge rat? I've heard of this. Janitor mob gone crazy.* They had been
awake for twenty-four hours. They took an eight-hour rest with four days and four hours left
to find the stairs.

Session ends there.
