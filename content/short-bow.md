From [[frank-doyle]]'s loot between sessions. A short bow is a two-handed weapon, so it
can't share his hands with the [[tire-iron]]. The GM's advice was to keep one in hand and
the other on the hotlist, so he can swap for free.

It uses the [[skill-bow|Bow]] skill. Its first outing, in
[[session-02]], came from the top of a stone pillar in the bone pit, after a fumbled
jump. It rolled a 4, and the bow ended up on the ground at his feet. Someone had to remind
him to pick it up afterwards.
