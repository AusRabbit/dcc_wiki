The AI's pop-up: *a stocky, broken-nosed leprechaun in a waistcoat two sizes too small,
cracking his knuckles around a blackthorn club. He guards the gold so that the clever ones
don't have to.* He opens with *to be sure, to be sure*.

They're tiny, but they hit like a truck.

## How they fight

- **Blackthorn club** — 1d10 + 3, bludgeoning. One cracked [[frank-doyle]] across the jowls for twenty while he was wearing his [[helm-of-the-bone-collector|bone helm]].
- **Quick** — six squares a move. They vault walls, and they'll spend a whole turn running to reach you. They flank, too: one worked round behind Frank like a cunning little bastard.
- **Pack** — they work in threes, often with a [[spud-mortar-leprechaun]] behind them. [[fergal-goldgrip]] whistles them in from the front of his house.
- **Bad at hiding** — one ran with the sisters' bone and left a trail of clover the whole way.
- **Vain** — flick a bruiser's top hat off and he worries about the hat more than the fight.
- **Bribes** — [[darcy-smith]] offered one a thousand gold for directions. It went badly.
