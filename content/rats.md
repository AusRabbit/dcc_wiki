The janitor mobs of the first floor. They eat whatever is left lying around, and they drag
the rest down to [[the-sump|the bone pit]].

[[mauritius]]'s warning: they're okay as long as there's something for them to eat. When
there isn't, they start on things they aren't supposed to eat.

## In the wild

One came out after the goblin fight in [[session-01]] and started eating a dead goblin.
[[the-goblin-prisoner]] worked out that the rats would know where things end up. The party
followed the stream of them to a broken sewer grate. They scattered from the torch.

## In the bone pit

[[the-bone-collector]] has duped the janitor mobs into serving it. The ones in its pit are
rabid. There's no flesh down there except what the party walks in with.

- **Bite** — 1d4 + 2. With 1 DR, a crawler with 2 health per slot usually loses one slot, sometimes none.
- **Swarming** — they flank, climb pillars after you, crawl up your legs and get under your clothes. One latched onto Frank under the poncho.
- **Reinforcements** — the Bone Collector roars and more come running. They can't attack on the turn they arrive.
- **Morale** — when the boss died, the survivors fled.

In [[session-02]], [[darcy-smith]] shot one into a spray of gore, [[michelle]] burned one to
cinders with Fire Fingers, and [[frank-doyle]] hacky-sacked one into the air and blew it
apart with the [[tire-iron]].

After the fight, more kept arriving to look for their leader, and the party were the only
meat in the room.
