[[darcy-smith]] had biscuits and dynamite on his hotlist, and in the middle of the fight with
[[the-bone-collector]] he put the two together. [[frank-doyle]] would like it noted that the
idea was his.

- **Made with** — Fabricate, on a Disadvantage roll because it was made mid-combat. It worked anyway. That used up his action, so he couldn't attack with it that turn.
- **Damage** — 1d8 + 2. Plain dynamite is 1d6.
- **Delivery** — Darcy climbed the boss and rammed it down its throat. That was an Explosives Handling check.

It did 10 damage and spilled the Bone Collector's entrails, but didn't kill it. The table had
expected more from dynamite in the stomach. The GM's answer: bigger explosives, lads.

> [!note] Strictly speaking
> Darcy pointed out afterwards that Fabricate can't make things with chemicals, so a bomb
> really needs Engineering or Explosives Handling. The GM allowed it anyway, because it was
> epic.

He kept experimenting with biscuit bombs while grinding on the way home, and picked up a
new skill to match at the safe room.
