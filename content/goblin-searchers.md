Funny little hats, big pipes, and magnifying glasses roughly the size of their own heads.
Between them they have spent eleven days on the case of the missing sisters, produced four
hundred pages of notes, and found exactly zero sisters.

They aren't looking for you. They will, however, work out exactly where you are hiding and
announce it at volume to everyone else in the room.

- **Attack** — the pipe is loaded with buckshot and doubles as a short-range pistol. It comes out with a cloud of smoke, and at least once it only made [[darcy-smith]] cough.
- **Behaviour** — slow to reload, shaky, not warriors.

## Encountered

Three of them, alongside three [[goblin-hunters]], on [[floor-01]]. [[darcy-smith]] shot two.
The third was too close to run, and is now [[the-goblin-prisoner]]. See [[session-01]].

They are working for a chief who wants the sisters put to rest.

> [!note] Searchers or Seekers
> The GM's description says *searcher*. The transcript also heard *seeker* at the table.
> Both forms resolve here.
