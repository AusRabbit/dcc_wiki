A $2 item that lives in a little plastic container for when it rains. [[darcy-smith]] got it
from a loot box in [[tutorial-guild]] and wore it out of there. [[mauritius]] assures him it
will help.

- **Slot** — torso.
- **Damage Resistance** — +1.

He looks a bit ridiculous in it and he rustles when he walks — in the GM's words, like a
human condom. It took the first point off the first boomerang of the campaign.

> [!note] Character sheet, not a computer game
> Putting armour in a slot doesn't update your DR. Add it to the armour box at the top of
> the sheet yourself.

## Handed down, then burned up

At the start of [[session-02]], Darcy upgraded to a [[motorcycle-jacket]] and gave the
poncho to [[frank-doyle]], who had nothing on his torso. It survived Frank falling onto a rib
cage in the bone pit, and the rats crawled under it to get at him.

It didn't survive Michelle. When her Fire Fingers set her alight, Frank put her out and the
poncho went up in flames, leaving him with burns across his chest. Delete it from your sheet.
