Was out for an evening dip with her cousin [[cheryl]] when the world flattened. Still in
her bathers. [[dale-prosky]] took her hostage in front of the stairs with a hand around her
neck, and she was still in the middle of the fight when it went down the stairs — she
grabbed at the [[sawn-off-shotgun]] mid-scrum and nearly got herself shot.

She came down with the party because she did not want to be alone, and because the two of
them were not the most frightening thing she had met that night. She has since been given a
club and told to defend herself, and has thought seriously about using it on
[[darcy-smith]] instead.

Not a player character. Played, in the GM's words, loosey-goosey.
