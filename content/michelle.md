A ballerina from Perth — *not that sort of dancer, mate*. Her sister [[cheryl]] lived in
Brisbane and invited her up to Noosa for a friend's hen's party. The two of them split from
the group after a few daiquiris to head home, and were by the water when the world
flattened. She was still in her bathers. She doesn't know where anyone else from the party
is.

[[dale-prosky]] took her hostage in front of the stairs with a hand around her neck. She
grabbed at the [[sawn-off-shotgun]] in the scrum and nearly got herself shot. She came down
with the party because she didn't want to be alone, and because the two of them weren't the
most frightening thing she'd met that night.

She knows what Frank's skull means and isn't frightened of it. If
anything she's grateful: they saved her, and in a way they got revenge for Cheryl.

## In the dungeon

[[mauritius]] gave her a long shoulder massage and a lot of whispered advice. She carries the
[[nailed-two-by-four]], and in [[session-01]] she missed with it, broke a goblin hunter's leg
with it, and then knocked the same goblin's head clean off, which surprised her as much as
anyone. Gunshots still make her scream. Magic missiles, oddly, don't. She is cold, she isn't
impressed by sewers, and she stays very close once the lights go down.

Not a player character. In the GM's words, she's played loosey-goosey, so she won't slow
combat down.
