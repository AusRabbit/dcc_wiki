Six foot of bat-like woman with sharp pointy teeth, who lives on the ceiling of
[[tutorial-guild]] and lands with a soft thud. A level 50 guide, and very excited you made
it. She is keen on the killing you've already done and the things you've already taken,
and she licked her lips at Frank's skull.

She walked the party through loot boxes, armour and skills in [[session-01]], gave
[[michelle]] a long shoulder massage, and told [[darcy-smith]] to put Frank at the front of
the battle line. After four hours she threw them out: there are many doors, many entrances,
and this isn't preschool.

## How she works at the table

Any time the GM has rules advice to give, it comes as a flashback to a conversation with
Mauritius in the safe room. That way nobody spends play time sitting in the safe room. You
can flash back to her with questions too.

## What she's told you so far

- *Read it, read it, read it* — about the spell book. Then *blow their fucking brains out*.
- Every crawler has a shitty little spell called Heal. Use your mana for what it's for, rather than dying trying to wrap a busted arm in a sling.
- Keep your hotlist full — ten slots. Anything not on it costs time to dig out mid-fight.
- The rats are janitor mobs. Fine while there's something to eat. When there isn't, they eat things they shouldn't.
- Don't drink the [[potion-of-skill]] early.
