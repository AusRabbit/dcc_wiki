Looted from [[the-bone-collector]] in [[session-02]]. When it was picked up, the minimap
spread out fast, showing the layout of the neighbourhood's corridors and rooms.

- **Map** — the boss's neighbourhood is added to your minimap, along with every mob in it, shown as simple coloured dots.
- **Grinding** — grind for 5 hours or more and you get credit for one extra hour. Five hours of grinding counts as six. See [[levelling-skills]].

Defeating a neighbourhood boss always gets you one. Tougher bosses drop better maps.
