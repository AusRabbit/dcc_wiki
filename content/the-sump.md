The first place the party saw the dungeon's layout change. In the middle of the crisscrossing
corridors, there's a sewer grate built into the wall with its bars ripped open, and the
[[rats]] come and go through it. Behind the grate it's cramped and dark, and it slopes down
to a sub-level of the floor.

The sub-level is a quintessential sewer, full of filth. At the bottom, over a ledge, is the
pit: about forty metres across, circular, and sunken into the ground. Everything the janitor
mobs have dragged off [[floor-01]] ends up here, and most of it is bones — big ones, small
ones, ones you don't recognise. It looks like they've been picking the world clean for a
long, long time.

## The boss battle

Stepping into the pit starts it. In [[session-02]], [[frank-doyle]] fell in when the
crumbling brickwork at the edge gave way. Time froze, *BOSS BATTLE* streamed across the HUD,
and [[the-bone-collector]] erupted out of the bones. Portcullis grates sealed the exits until
the boss was dead.

The pit has stone pillars and platforms to climb, and deep drifts of bone. Darcy ended up
chest-deep in them at one point. It drops away from the rim, but the fall is nothing serious.

## The bones

[[the-goblin-prisoner]] looked down and said they just had to find *their* bones. After the
fight, the party spent four hours searching. Darcy's two hours of tracking turned up a small
bone that the goblin called *could be, not sure*. Frank's dumpster dive found one of the
bones.
