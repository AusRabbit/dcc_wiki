The first time the party saw the dungeon's layout change. In the middle of the crisscrossing
corridors there's a sewer grate built into the wall with its bars ripped open, and the
[[rats]] come and go through it. Behind the grate it's cramped and dark and slopes down to
a sub-level of the floor.

The sub-level is a quintessential sewer, full of filth. At the bottom, over a ledge, is a
sump: a pit holding all the refuse, bile and waste that the janitor mobs have dragged off
[[floor-01]].

The most interesting thing in it is bones. Lots and lots of bones.

[[the-goblin-prisoner]] looked down and said they just had to find *their* bones.
