[[frank-doyle]]'s new main-hand weapon, from loot between sessions. It's non-magical and does
1d6 + Strength, but it uses the [[skill-quarterstaff|Quarterstaff]] skill, not Club, which
surprised Frank.

Its finest moment came in [[session-02]]. A rat was gnawing on Frank's toe. He kicked it up
to eye level like a hacky sack, spent an AI favour on the reroll, and swung. The rat
exploded into a thousand pieces.

Frank argued that it counts as a club. The GM checked, and it's Quarterstaff.
