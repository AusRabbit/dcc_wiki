The leprechauns' artillery. You hear it before you see it: a thump, a puff of smoke from the
corner of the room, then a whistle as the shell comes down on your position.

- **Shell** — the potato bursts on impact and throws out molten chunks that go through barriers and stick in the dungeon walls. If you're vulnerable to bludgeoning, the GM was clear it would *really* ruin your day. The opening blast at [[the-graves]] did 1d6 + 3 to each crawler and threw them both off the hill.
- **Range** — it sits back and fires from the far corner while the [[shillelagh-bruiser|bruisers]] close in.
- **Ammo crate** — it keeps its shells in a crate beside it. That's its weakness.

## Encountered

[[session-03]]. At the graves, [[darcy-smith]] hit the ammo crate and the whole corner went up.
The leprechaun turned into mashed potato, and there was nothing left to loot. Another one was
stationed in the fields of the [[leprechaun-quarter]].
