First heard as children's laughter coming from ahead and behind at the same time. Twice
glimpsed in the middle distance as two figures in white, side by side, gone on a second
look.

Seen properly after [[the-wall-of-blood]]: two green-skinned girls in pristine white
dresses, walking forward in perfect unison, spotless in a corridor running with blood. The
laughter does not match the serious look on their faces.

The party ran.
