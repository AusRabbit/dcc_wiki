First heard as children's laughter coming from ahead and behind at the same time. Twice
glimpsed in the middle distance as two figures in white, side by side, gone on a second
look.

Seen properly after [[the-wall-of-blood]]: two green-skinned girls in pristine white
dresses, walking forward in perfect unison, spotless in a corridor running with blood. The
laughter does not match the serious look on their faces.

The party ran.

## Since then

The GM's recap at the start of [[session-01]] described them as dungeon interlopers *that
threw a wall of blood at you*. So the wall may be theirs.

The [[goblin-searchers]] have spent eleven days on *the case of the missing sisters*. The
sisters are revered and worshipped, and they *shouldn't be up*. As
[[the-goblin-prisoner]] was explaining this, schoolgirls giggled somewhere in the distance.
The prisoner is very glad to be getting away from that sound. Nobody has said the girls and
the sisters are the same.
