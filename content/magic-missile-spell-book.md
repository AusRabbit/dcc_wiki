From [[frank-doyle]]'s loot boxes. He only remembered he had it partway through the first goblin
fight. In the flashback [[mauritius]] got very excited about it — *read it, read it, read it*.
Reading it uses the book up and teaches the spell.

[[spell-magic-missile]] at **rank 3**:

- **Mana** — 5. Frank has 6, so it's one cast and then he's out until he rests or drinks a potion.
- **Range** — line of sight.
- **Damage** — 1d4 + Int, force. Frank is an Int build, at +3.

The first cast came out of his eyes before he knew he'd fired it, and turned a
[[goblin-hunters|goblin hunter]] into red pulp. His first spell is Magic Missile, the same as
Donut's in the books. The GM says that was the System AI, not him.
