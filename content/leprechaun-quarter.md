The leprechaun neighbourhood. It runs a drug trade and enslaves other mobs to work for it —
mainly goblins. Corridors cross and wind over each other, climbing and dropping, with chain
gangs of prisoners marched along them under guard into the middle of the neighbourhood.

## The fields

In the centre is an enormous, cavernous room with a layer of dirt on the floor. The
leprechauns have turned it into farmland. Goblins work it like slaves, overseen by ordinary
leprechauns in their Sunday best, growing what passes for weed in the dungeon — the devil's
lettuce, as it will forever be known.

There are [[shillelagh-bruiser|shillelagh bruisers]] on the balcony and a
[[spud-mortar-leprechaun]] off to one side. Important guests arrive by horse and cart.

In [[session-03]], [[frank-doyle]] set the crop on fire as a distraction. In the chaos, some of
the goblins slipped away and the leprechauns ran to save their lettuce. By the time the party
fled, the overseers had whipped the goblins back into line with whips, knuckle dusters and
clover-leaf magic. A few watched them go with sunken, hollow eyes.

## The mansion

[[fergal-goldgrip]]'s house sits in the middle of the fields. It has two storeys, polished
wooden stairs, guards at the front door and a vase of flowers on the entry table — until the
bruisers knock it over. His office is at the bottom of the stairs, full of scattered gold, with
a library whose shelves hold pots of gold instead of books.
