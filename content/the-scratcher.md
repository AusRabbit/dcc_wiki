A one-use scratch card from [[frank-doyle]]'s loot. Scratch it in combat and roll 1d2:

- **1 — Lube.** One target within 20 feet is covered in lube and gains the Staggered debuff.
- **2 — Medicine.** The target gets a level 15 Heal Other.

At one health slot in [[session-03]], Frank scratched it on himself, hoping for the heal. He
rolled a 1. It was as if a dump truck had emptied a load of KY jelly over him. Frank, the
[[shillelagh-bruiser|shillelagh bruisers]] and the hill at [[the-graves]] all disappeared under a
torrent of ooey-gooey, slippery lubricant, and everyone went sliding off the hill.

It didn't stop there. Hours later, the lube was still on his shoes when he stepped onto the
polished stairs of [[fergal-goldgrip]]'s mansion, and he went all the way down on his backside.
