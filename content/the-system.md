Five characteristics — strength, dexterity, constitution, intelligence, charisma — each with
a modifier that applies to every roll of that type. A skill check is d20 + skill rank +
modifier. The GM sets the difficulty secretly and tells you how well you did, not the number.
If you don't have the skill, you can usually still try it, with Disadvantage.

Health is a bar of ten slots, tracked on the dashboard. Mana pays for spells, and it only
comes back when you rest, drink a potion or get the right buff. Time passing out of combat
restores a little of both.

Safe rooms are where achievements, loot boxes and [[levelling-skills|skill levelling]]
happen, and where you can take a short rest and heal. Advancing time — resting, looting,
crafting — costs hours off the floor's countdown.

## Your sheet

- **You're the master of your own sheet.** It's a character sheet, not a computer game. Put armour in a slot and you still have to add its DR at the top yourself.
- **Hotlist** — ten slots for the things you need quickly. Spells, weapons and potions go here.
- **Inventory** — click an item to expand it. It can be sorted by type or A–Z. Right-click to send an item to a mate.
- **Party tab** — the GM's suggestion for actually playing. The character tab is for managing your sheet between games.
- **Weapon badges** — left-click rolls to hit, right-click rolls damage.
