Granted regency over the solar system by [[the-syndicate]] after humanity failed to file a
proper appeal for its own mineral and elemental rights within fifty solars of first contact.
Having crushed the interior of the world and begun mining it, Borant was entitled to choose
the manner in which surviving humans could reclaim their lost matter.

It chose option three: [[the-world-dungeon]].

Its announcements arrive as floating text in front of your face, in letters that crawl
downward rather than across, in a language you have never learned and can read perfectly.
