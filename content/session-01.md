The party woke up in [[tutorial-guild]] with a silver skull hanging over [[frank-doyle]]'s
head, left four hours later with their first armour and a spell book, and ended the night
standing over a pit of bones with a goblin on a rope.

## The Tutorial Guild

[[mauritius]] — six foot of bat, level 50, all sharp pointy teeth — unfolded from the
ceiling and congratulated them on making it. Her eyes lingered on [[frank-doyle]]. Through
the new HUD overlay both crawlers could see why: a silver player-killer skull next to Frank's
name, earned for [[dale-prosky]] at the bottom of the stairs. She licked her lips and called
it some kind of record. A few seasons ago, she said, the first two people into the dungeon
had juked it out.

She walked them through their loot boxes. [[darcy-smith]] put on
[[moms-emergency-poncho]] and immediately rustled. Frank strapped on a pair of
[[double-pluggers]]. Then came the first harvest of skills, done the tutorial way
([[levelling-skills]]): Frank kept Deception, Darcy kept Climbing, Frank's Club, Endurance
and Stealth went up, and Darcy's Shotgun reached rank 5 and started doing 2d10.

Mauritius gave [[michelle]] a long shoulder massage and a lot of whispered advice, and
quietly told Darcy to put Frank at the front of the battle line. After four hours she
pushed them out. *This isn't preschool.* The door closed behind them, and the floor
countdown appeared in their HUDs.

## Small Talk in the Corridors

Dried blood underfoot from [[the-wall-of-blood]]. Cheering somewhere in the distance. Caged
doorways, barricades, and intersections that all looked the same.

Darcy told them he used to ride motorcycles and knows people — which is how a trucker gets
a [[sawn-off-shotgun]]. Frank is from Sydney. Michelle is from Perth, and a ballerina — not
that sort of dancer, she told Darcy, who had clearly been about to ask. Her sister
[[cheryl]] lived in Brisbane and had brought her up to Noosa for a friend's hen's party.
They split from the group after a few daiquiris to walk home. She doesn't know where
anyone is now, and she cried a little, thinking about Cheryl. Darcy apologised for the
nickname, and then didn't stop using it.

## The Goblins

An hour in, an intersection with movement in the dark: nails on stone, rubble being
shifted, little voices. Frank wanted to sneak up and look. He slipped into cover; Darcy
didn't, and was spotted with Michelle in tow.

Six goblins — three [[goblin-hunters]] with boomerangs and three [[goblin-searchers]]
with pipes and magnifying glasses. All six went for Darcy.

Frank stood up and shouted *Look at me, you ugly goblins!* The taunt didn't land. Darcy
dodged two blasts of buckshot from the searchers' pipes and took a boomerang to the side of
the head. Then Frank remembered he was holding a spell book, Mauritius's voice came back to
him — *read it, read it, read it* — and a Magic Missile came out of his eyes. The nearest
hunter turned into red pulp and showered the goblin behind him. Mauritius's voice again, in
Darcy's head this time: *every crawler has a shitty little spell called Heal*. Darcy got up
off the ground and shot a searcher in the chest from close range.

The fight went three rounds. Michelle missed with the [[nailed-two-by-four]], then broke a
hunter's leg with it, then took the same hunter's head clean off and surprised herself.
Darcy blew a second searcher apart. Frank's paddle bounced off a crockery helmet, twice. A
boomerang caught Frank square in the chest. Darcy tried a taunt of his own; the goblins
laughed at him.

The last hunter ran screaming into the tunnels. It will probably be back.

## Please Kindly, Sir

The last searcher was too close to Darcy to run. Darcy told him to put his little bombs
down and rolled a natural 20 to intimidate him. With trembling hands the goblin held out a
warrant. It was written in a script Darcy had never seen and could somehow read: their
chief had sent the searchers into the tunnels to recover *that which is most precious*, to
put the sisters at rest.

The sisters are revered. The goblins worship them. *They shouldn't be up.* The signs, he
said, are everywhere — here, there, above and below. And right then, somewhere in the
distance, schoolgirls giggled.

## Follow the Rats

Four bodies looted for [[miscellaneous-junk]]. Darcy cast Heal on himself and tossed his
mana potion to Frank. The prisoner looked over his dead colleagues: that one had the best
lead, and that one was about to tell them something important.

A rat scampered out and began eating a dead goblin. *The rats will know*, the goblin said —
the rats eat things. Mauritius's advice about the [[rats]] came back to them: fine as long
as there's something for them to eat. Frank followed them back to their source, untrained,
on a very good roll. They tied the prisoner up with rope and walked him along. He steered
them away from bad turnings and proudly warned them off his own trap, a stick propped up on
another stick.

The rats led to a sewer grate in the wall with its bars ripped open: cramped, dark, and
sloping down. Frank's plan was to squeeze through with the goblin and, if it got dicey,
leave him for the rats. They spent an hour and two pieces of junk making a torch instead.
The rats fled from the light.

Below, past a lot of filth and with Michelle staying very close, they came to a ledge over
[[the-sump]] — everything the janitor mobs have dragged off the first floor, and in it,
bones. Lots and lots of bones.

The goblin looked down. *Excellent. We just have to find their bones.*

Session ends there.
