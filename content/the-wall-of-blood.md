Announced by a rumbling in the floor, then thirty feet of blood filling the corridor wall to
wall at a great rate of knots, stripping lichen off the stone as it goes.

[[darcy-smith]] rolled a natural twenty, climbed the wall, caught hold of a brazier and held
on with [[michelle]] over his shoulder. [[frank-doyle]] tried to surf it, ate a wall, and
came out coughing thick mucusy blood.

It subsides on its own. The GM was clear that it comes and goes.
