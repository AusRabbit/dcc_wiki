The hour between the collapse and the stairwells sealing. Buildings gone, the interior of
the world crushed, hundreds of injured and dead on the sand at Noosa, and a single
ethereal light at the far end of the strip marking the way down.

[[borant-corporation]]'s announcement gave everyone the same choice and the same deadline.
Most people ran the other way, towards homes and families that no longer existed.
