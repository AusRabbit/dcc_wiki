The System AI's introduction: the janitor mobs on this floor have been duped into serving
the Bone Collector. This giant rat seems older and wiser than the others, and it's a master
of surprise. It likes to lure victims into its pit and devour them. It doesn't like crawlers
who steal from it, either — old-school morals, apparently.

Stands taller than a human, like a bear on its hind legs, with bones sticking out of its
body and certain shaped bones worn as armour. It howls with a weird hooting, bleating noise.
In the GM's words, it isn't the smartest of creatures.

## How it fights

- **Burrow** — it vanishes into the bones and comes up somewhere else.
- **Bone eruption** — it explodes out of the ground beside a target and throws up a frag zone of splintered bone. This is an area attack.
- **Slam / bite** — 1d6+3. It slammed [[darcy-smith]] into the wall and left him Staggered (his next action couldn't be a move).
- **Toss** — 1d4+2. It threw Darcy off its back and deep into the bones.
- **Call the rats** — it roars and more [[rats]] come running. They can't attack on the turn they arrive.
- **Its pit** — stepping into [[the-sump|the bone pit]] starts the boss battle. Portcullis grates seal the exits until the boss is dead.
