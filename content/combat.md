Unlike most systems, the GM tells you what the enemy is about to do and you respond — the
way a video game telegraphs an attack, and the way the books work.

- **Two actions per round**, per crawler.
- **Step** — every action carries a free step of two squares (10 feet) with it.
- **Move** — 20 feet, four squares. Move plus step is 30 feet in one action.
- **Evade** — d20 plus your evade modifier against the incoming attack. Spending an action to
  evade is a real cost. Fail and you take it.
- **Intervene** — spend an action to add 1d6 to an ally's near-miss and try to lift it into a
  success.
- **Natural 20** is a triumph. You narrate why it worked.

Each square on the battle map is five feet, so divide every range by five.

Enemy dice are rolled by the Dungeon AI.
