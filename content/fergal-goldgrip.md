The AI's introduction: *a natty leprechaun in a three-piece suit of emerald tweed, with
gold-rimmed spectacles and a ledger chained to his wrist. Behind him, at the very end of a
flickering holographic rainbow, sits the pot: every coin anyone on this floor ever owed him.*

He runs the [[leprechaun-quarter]] from a two-storey mansion in the middle of his fields,
from an office strewn with gold. Its library shelves are stacked with pots of gold rather
than books. *We're not just businessmen.*

## How he fights

- **Delegates** — he has every confidence his [[shillelagh-bruiser|shillelagh bruisers]] can handle you. He puts two fingers in his mouth and whistles, and the bruisers from the front of the house pile in.
- **Cover** — he stays behind his desk. Attack him from the doorway and you have Disadvantage. When he leans out to cast, you get Advantage.
- **Rainbow Repossession** — a kitsch spray of coloured sparkles. Fail to evade it and it repossesses what's in your hands. It took [[sawn-off-shotgun|Betsy]] and Frank's [[canoe-paddle]] and flung them across the room. He can throw it at more than one crawler a round.

## Session 03

[[frank-doyle]] fell down Fergal's staircase and started the boss battle. A Magic Missile
brought his gold shelves down on top of him, but neither crawler landed a hit. After the
repossession, the GM offered a way out: Frank threw four thousand gold in the air, and while
every leprechaun scrambled for it, [[darcy-smith]] grabbed Betsy back.

**Fergal still has:** Frank's canoe paddle, the four thousand gold, and the sisters' bones. One of his
bruisers dug them up at [[the-graves]] and brought them straight here.
