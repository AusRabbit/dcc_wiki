The light at the far end of the strip, visible from the beach and further away than it
looked. Tens of people gathered near it in the dark, and a small heap of bodies lay at its
foot courtesy of [[dale-prosky]].

Below it, a short corridor ending in a ginormous ornate set of doors worked in metallic
relief with something that could be described as demonic, or possibly aquatic. The doorway
crashed closed behind the party as they crossed into [[floor-01]].
