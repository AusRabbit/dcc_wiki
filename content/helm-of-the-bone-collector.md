A helmet made from the [[bone-carapace]] looted off [[the-bone-collector]]. It has no special
name. It's [[frank-doyle]]'s.

- **+1 Dexterity.**
- **Piercing resistance** — half damage from piercing.
- **Bludgeoning vulnerability** — double damage from bludgeoning.

Its first outing went badly. The opening explosion at [[the-graves]] counted as bludgeoning, so
Frank took double, and then a [[shillelagh-bruiser]]'s club cracked him across the jowls for
twenty, leaving him on one health slot. In the GM's words, it's a poisoned chalice. Frank took
it off mid-fight and lost the Dexterity, the resistance and the vulnerability along with it.
