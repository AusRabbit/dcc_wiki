From Sydney. Came up to Noosa for the weekend off from his hard life as a barista, to clear
his head about dropping out of university and whether to go back. Decided within about
ninety seconds of the apocalypse that he had been gaming his whole life for this exact
moment.

Smarter than his companion and considerably weaker — an Int build at intelligence +3, with
evade +2 and a strength he describes as *not that strong*. He wears a
silver player-killer skull for [[dale-prosky]]. He says it was a team effort.

## Kit

- [[paddle-club]] — still his melee weapon, and still bouncing off goblin helmets.
- [[magic-missile-spell-book]] — read mid-fight. Magic Missile at rank 3, 5 mana a cast, and he has 6.
- [[double-pluggers]] — feet, +1 DR.
- A mana potion, tossed to him by [[darcy-smith]] after the goblin fight. It's on his hotlist.
- A blueberry-ice vape and a water bottle ([[oddments]]).
