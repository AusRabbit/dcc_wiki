Came up to Noosa for the weekend to clear his head about dropping out of university and
whether to go back. Decided within about ninety seconds of the apocalypse that he had been
gaming his whole life for this exact moment.

Smarter than his companion and considerably weaker — intelligence plus three, evade plus
two, and a strength score he describes as "not that strong". Fights with a [[paddle-club]],
and looted [[dale-prosky]]'s [[nailed-two-by-four]] on the way down the stairs.

Carries a blueberry-ice vape and a water bottle. Called the party's first play and has been
calling them since.
