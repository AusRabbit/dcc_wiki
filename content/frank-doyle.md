From Sydney. Came up to Noosa for the weekend off from his hard life as a barista, to clear
his head about dropping out of university and whether to go back. Decided within about
ninety seconds of the apocalypse that he had been gaming his whole life for this exact
moment.

Smarter than his companion and considerably weaker — an Int build, with a strength he
describes as *not that strong*. He wears a
silver player-killer skull for [[dale-prosky]]. He says it was a team effort.

## Kit

- [[tire-iron]] — main hand.
- [[short-bow]] — two-handed. He picked it back up off the bone-pit floor.
- [[paddle-club]] — still on his attack bar.
- Magic Missile — learned from the [[magic-missile-spell-book]].
- [[double-pluggers]] — feet, +1 DR.
- A poison antidote. He gave the other to [[darcy-smith]].
- A vape ([[oddments]]). He tossed it to Darcy after the boss died. His water went over Michelle.
