The place [[the-goblin-prisoner]] wouldn't go back to. *Last time we went to the graves,
things got wild.*

A large room with corridors leading in and out. Dappled sunlight falls on it from nowhere in
particular, and something like church organ music drifts in as you enter. In the middle is a
hill of graves. The two in the centre carry the same words, beautifully carved by hand in
letters you shouldn't be able to read but can: *taken before their time*. Around the edges,
the other tombs suggest a family plot for important people.

Every grave had been dug up and its contents removed. There was a lot of activity around the
graveside.

## Session 03

[[darcy-smith]] picked the grave that looked the most disturbed, put the sisters' bone in it
and gave it a proper burial. Then an explosion from a [[spud-mortar-leprechaun]] threw both
crawlers off the hill, and [[shillelagh-bruiser|shillelagh bruisers]] attacked. While the
party fought, the bruisers dug the bone back up and carried it off to [[fergal-goldgrip]].

When they did, the sunlight went out, and the giggling started — with every third or fourth
giggle a scream. See [[the-white-dress-girls]].
