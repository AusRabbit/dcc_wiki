Two feet of timber with gnarly bits of nail sticking out of it. Killed [[cheryl]] and at
least one other person, and opened [[frank-doyle]]'s neck for eight damage on the first
swing of the campaign.

[[frank-doyle]] picked it up off [[dale-prosky]]'s body at the bottom of [[the-stairwell]],
and the party lent it to [[michelle]]. In [[session-01]] she broke a goblin hunter's leg with
it, then took the same goblin's head off.
